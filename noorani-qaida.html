What Is Noorani Qaida?

Noorani Qaida is a foundational learning method designed to help beginners learn how to read Arabic correctly and prepare for reading the Holy Quran.

At Al Shimaa Quran Academy, students learn Noorani Qaida through structured online lessons with qualified Quran teachers who guide them step by step.

Why Learn Noorani Qaida?

Learn Arabic Letters

Build a strong understanding of Arabic letters and their correct pronunciation.

Improve Pronunciation

Practice the correct sounds and pronunciation needed for Quran reading.

Prepare for Quran Reading

Develop the essential reading skills needed before progressing to Quran recitation.

Perfect for Beginners

A suitable starting point for children and beginners with little or no previous experience.

What Will Students Learn?

Arabic alphabet and letter recognition
Correct pronunciation of Arabic letters
Arabic letters with basic vowel sounds
Tanween and basic reading patterns
Madd and elongation
Sukoon and Shaddah
Reading Arabic words step by step
Applying reading skills to Quranic words
