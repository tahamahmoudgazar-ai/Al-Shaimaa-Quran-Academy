Learn Islamic Studies Online

Our Islamic Studies course helps students understand the fundamental teachings of Islam in a simple, structured and practical way.

Students learn about Islamic beliefs, worship, manners, the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and other essential areas of Islamic knowledge.

Lessons are delivered online by qualified teachers and can be adapted to the student's age, level and learning goals.

What Will You Learn?

  • Islamic beliefs and basic Aqeedah
  • The Five Pillars of Islam
  • Salah and the basics of worship
  • Islamic manners and daily etiquette
  • Seerah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
  • Stories of the Prophets
  • Islamic values and character
  • Essential knowledge for everyday Muslim life
