What is Ijazah?

Ijazah is an official certification that you have mastered Quran recitation with perfect Tajweed. Without Ijazah you can teach, but with Ijazah your certificate is recognized worldwide and you join chain of scholars back to Prophet ﷺ. Learn basics first from what Tajweed is and why it is important.

Types of Ijazah

1. Ijazah in Hafs An Asim

Most common recitation worldwide. You recite full Quran to teacher with Tajweed.

2. Ijazah in Tajweed Rules

Theory + application. For teachers who want to teach Tajweed itself.

3. Ijazah in Hifz (Memorization)

You recite full Quran from memory. Requires strong revision like our Hifz guide and how long memorization takes.

Who Can Apply in USA & UK?

Ideal for students who already completed learning Quran online and improved reading. If you are beginner, start with Noorani Qaida then Tajweed.

How Long Does Ijazah Take Online?

6-12 months with 2-3 classes/week. EST/PST/GMT flexible. Depends on your current level and daily practice. Similar to Juz Amma plan but more advanced.
