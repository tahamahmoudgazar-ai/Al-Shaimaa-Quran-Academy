What is Ijazah?

Ijazah is an official certification that you have mastered Quran recitation with perfect Tajweed and memorization. There are 3 main types: Ijazah in Hafs An Asim, Ijazah in Tajweed Rules, and Ijazah in Hifz.

Who Can Apply in USA & UK?

  • Students who read Quran fluently with Tajweed
  • Adults in USA/UK wanting to teach family or community
  • Teachers seeking higher credibility
