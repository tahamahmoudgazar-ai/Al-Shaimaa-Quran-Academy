What is Zakat in Islam? Complete Guide to Nisab, Calculation & 8 Categories

Your complete guide to Zakat - the 3rd Pillar of Islam. Learn Nisab threshold 2026, how to calculate 2.5%, Zakat on gold, savings, 401k, stocks, and who receives Zakat according to Quran 9:60.

What is Zakat? Definition and Importance

Zakat is the 3rd Pillar of Islam and an obligatory charity on eligible wealth. Linguistically, Zakat means purification and growth. By giving 2.5% of excess wealth, your wealth is purified and blessed by Allah.

Allah says: "Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them and cause them to increase" Quran 9:103

Zakat is not optional charity like Sadaqah - it is Fard for every Muslim whose wealth exceeds Nisab and has passed one lunar year (Hawl).

Zakat Nisab Threshold 2026 - How Much Wealth Requires Zakat?

Nisab is the minimum threshold. If your wealth is below Nisab, no Zakat is due.

Current Nisab Values (May 2026):

  • Silver Nisab: 612.36g silver = approx $550 - $650 (most scholars use this - more beneficial for poor)
  • Gold Nisab: 85g gold = approx $5,500 - $6,000

Most contemporary scholars in USA & UK advise using Silver Nisab to benefit more poor people. So if you have $600+ savings for 1 lunar year, you pay Zakat.

How to Calculate Zakat: 2.5% Formula Made Easy

Simple Formula: Total Zakatable Wealth x 0.025 = Zakat Due

What Counts as Zakatable Wealth?

  • Cash savings in bank, at home
  • Gold and silver jewelry (above personal use - scholars differ)
  • Stocks, shares, crypto held for investment
  • 401k, IRA, retirement accounts (if accessible)
  • Rental property income saved, business inventory
  • Money lent to others expected back

Example Calculation:

You have: $10,000 savings + $3,000 gold + $5,000 stocks = $18,000
Zakat = $18,000 x 2.5% = $450

Zakat on Gold, Silver, Money, 401k, and Stocks

Zakat on Gold:

85g+ gold kept as wealth. If jewelry is for daily personal use, some madhabs exempt it. If stored as investment, pay Zakat.

Zakat on 401k & IRA (USA Muslims):

If you can withdraw with penalty, pay Zakat on full balance. If locked till retirement, many scholars say pay when you receive it. Consult scholar for your situation.

Zakat on Stocks:

Pay on market value if bought to sell. If long-term dividend investment, pay on dividends saved, some say 2.5% on 25-30% of share value representing zakatable assets of company.

8 Categories of Zakat Recipients - Quran 9:60

Allah specified exactly 8 categories:

  1. Al-Fuqara: The poor with almost nothing
  2. Al-Masakin: The needy who don't have enough
  3. Amilina Alayha: Zakat administrators
  4. Muallafatul Quloob: Those whose hearts are to be reconciled
  5. Ar-Riqab: Freeing slaves / helping those in bondage
  6. Al-Gharimin: Those in debt
  7. Fi Sabeelillah: In the path of Allah (students, dawah)
  8. Ibn As-Sabeel: Stranded travelers

When to Pay Zakat? Hawl - The Lunar Year

Wealth must be above Nisab for one full Islamic lunar year (354 days). You can pay anytime after Hawl completes. Many Muslims choose Ramadan for extra reward.

Best practice: Set a Zakat anniversary date (e.g., 1st Ramadan) and calculate every year on that date.

Common Questions About Zakat

FAQ 1: Do I pay Zakat if I have debt?

Deduct immediate debts from wealth, then calculate. Long-term mortgage not fully deducted - only one year payment according to many scholars.

FAQ 2: Can I give Zakat to family?

You can give to brother, sister, aunt, uncle if they are poor, but NOT to parents, grandparents, children, grandchildren, or spouse - you must support them anyway.

FAQ 3: Can I pay Zakat monthly?

Yes, you can pay in advance and adjust at Hawl date. If you overpaid, it's Sadaqah. If underpaid, pay difference.

FAQ 4: Zakat on house I live in?

No Zakat on personal home, car, furniture you use.
