What is Qalqala in Tajweed? Rules, Letters & Examples (Easy Guide)

One of the first Tajweed rules every beginner learns is Qalqala. If your Quran reading sounds flat or letters disappear, Qalqala is often the missing sound. In this easy guide, we explain Qalqala letters, types, and examples.

What is Qalqala in Tajweed?

Qalqala means "bouncing" or "echo" sound. When one of the 5 Qalqala letters has Sukoon (no vowel), you make a light bounce so the letter is heard clearly. Without Qalqala, the letter sound gets lost.

What Are the 5 Qalqala Letters? (Qutb Jad)

Easy to remember: ق ط ب ج د = Qutb Jad (قطب جد)

  • ق - Qaf
  • ط - Ta
  • ب - Ba
  • ج - Jeem
  • د - Dal

3 Types of Qalqala

1. Qalqala Sughra (Small) - In the middle of word

Example: يَقْطَعون - Qaf in middle with Sukoon, light bounce.

2. Qalqala Wusta (Medium) - At end of word but you continue

Example: قَدْ أَفْلَحَ - Dal at end, medium bounce.

3. Qalqala Kubra (Biggest) - At end with Shaddah or Waqf

Example: الْحَقُّ - Strong bounce at stop.

Common Mistakes in Qalqala

  • ❌ Moving the letter (adding a vowel) - Qalqala is not a vowel
  • ❌ Too strong bounce like a full vowel
  • ❌ Forgetting Qalqala at all

Examples of Qalqala from Quran

  • قَدْ - Qalqala on Dal
  • يَجْعَل - Qalqala on Jeem
  • مَقْعَد - Qalqala on Qaf
  • أَطْعَمَهُم - Qalqala on Ta
  • أَبْصَار - Qalqala on Ba
