What Is Izhar in Tajweed? Letters, Rule & Quran Examples

Izhar means to pronounce Noon Sakinah or Tanween clearly without Ghunnah. When one of 6 throat letters comes after Noon, you make Izhar. Easiest rule to start Tajweed for USA & UK beginners.

What Is Izhar? Definition

Izhar = to make clear. Pronounce Noon clearly from its Makharij, no hiding, no merging, no extra Ghunnah.

Izhar Letters - 6 Throat Letters

ء ه ع ح غ خ - Hamza, Ha, Ayn, Ha, Ghayn, Kha - all from throat (Halq)

Sentence: أخي هاك علما حازه غير خاسر

Izhar Examples

  • مِنْ خَوْفٍ → Min-khawf - clear Noon
  • مَنْ آمَنَ → Man-aamana
  • عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ → Aleemun-Hakeem
