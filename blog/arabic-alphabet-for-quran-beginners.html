Arabic Alphabet for Quran Beginners: 7-Day Guide

Arabic Basics

28 letters = key to Quran. Learning Arabic alphabet is first step for any beginner in USA & UK. With correct Makharij (articulation points), you can start reading Quran in one week.

The 28 Letters - Grouped by Shape

We teach letters in groups that look similar: Ba-Ta-Tha, Jeem-Ha-Kha. Easier to memorize than alphabetical order.

3 Forms of Each Letter

  • Beginning: بـ
  • Middle: ـبـ
  • End: ـب

Makharij - Where Sound Comes From

Qaf from deepest throat, Ba from lips. Our teachers show mouth position on camera.

7-Day Plan

Day 1-2: Alif to Kha, Day 3-4: Dal to Fa, Day 5-6: Qaf to Ya, Day 7: Revision + joining letters. Free PDF chart included.
