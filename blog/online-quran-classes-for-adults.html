Online Quran Classes for Adults in USA, UK & Worldwide

Whether you are starting from Arabic letters, want to improve your recitation, study Tajweed, memorize Quran, or learn Islamic studies, your teacher can build a practical learning path for you.

Quran Classes for Adults: Choose Your Learning Path

1. Quran Reading for Beginners

If you cannot read Arabic yet, start with letters, pronunciation, joining letters, and a structured reading foundation. Our Noorani Qaida course can provide that foundation.

2. Tajweed & Recitation Correction

If you already read Quran but want better pronunciation and recitation, study Tajweed with direct teacher feedback. Explore our Tajweed course and read what Tajweed is and why it matters.

3. Quran Memorization

Adults can follow a manageable memorization and revision routine around work and family responsibilities. See our Quran memorization program.

4. Arabic Language & Islamic Studies

Students who want broader learning can also explore Arabic language and Islamic Studies.
