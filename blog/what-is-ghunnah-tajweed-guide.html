What Is Ghunnah in Tajweed? Rules & Examples

Ghunnah is a nasal sound that comes from nose, held for 2 counts (harakah). It happens in Noon Mushaddad (نّ) and Meem Mushaddad (مّ). For beginners USA & UK, Ghunnah is fun to learn after Qalqala.

Where Does Ghunnah Happen?

  • Noon Mushaddad نّ - e.g. إِنَّ - inna (hold 2 counts)
  • Meem Mushaddad مّ - e.g. ثُمَّ - thumma
  • Idgham with Ghunnah, Iqlab, Ikhfa - also have Ghunnah 2 counts (intermediate)

How Long Is Ghunnah?

Ghunnah is 2 harakah (counts) - about 2 seconds, nasal from nose. Teacher will help you feel it.
