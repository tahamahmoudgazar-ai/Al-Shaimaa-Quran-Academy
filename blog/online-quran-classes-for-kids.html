Online Quran Classes for Kids: What Your Child Can Learn

Every child starts at a different level. We can build a plan around the skills your child needs most.

  • Quran reading: Arabic letters, sounds, joining letters, and fluent reading.
  • Noorani Qaida: A structured foundation for children who are not yet ready to read Quran independently. See our Noorani Qaida course.
  • Tajweed: Pronunciation and recitation rules with direct correction from the teacher. Explore our Tajweed course.
  • Quran memorization: New memorization with regular revision through our Quran memorization program.
  • Islamic studies: Age-appropriate lessons can include essential Islamic knowledge through our Islamic Studies course.

Why One-on-One Quran Classes Work Well for Children

In a private live class, the teacher can focus on one child, listen to every recitation, correct mistakes, and adjust the lesson pace. This also allows parents to choose lesson times that fit school, family, and activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age can children start Quran classes?

The right starting point depends on the child's readiness and current skills. Noorani Qaida can be introduced to young learners who are ready to focus and participate in a lesson.

Can my child learn Quran from home?

Yes. Classes are live and online, allowing children to learn from home while interacting directly with their teacher.

Do you offer Quran memorization for kids?

Yes. Children can follow a structured memorization and revision plan through our Quran memorization program.
