Sifat Al-Huruf: Characteristics of Arabic Letters

Sifat Al-Huruf means the characteristics or qualities of Arabic letters. In Tajweed, learning these characteristics helps you pronounce every Arabic letter correctly and distinguish letters that may sound similar.

Quick Answer: What Is Sifat Al-Huruf?

Sifat Al-Huruf describes the qualities that accompany Arabic letters when they are pronounced. Important characteristics include Hams, Jahr, Shiddah, Rikhawah, Tawassut, Isti'la, Istifal, Itbaq, Infitah, and Qalqalah.

Understanding these qualities makes Quran recitation clearer, more accurate, and closer to proper Tajweed.

What Does Sifat Al-Huruf Mean?

The Arabic word Sifat (صفات) means characteristics or qualities, while Al-Huruf (الحروف) means letters. Therefore, Sifat Al-Huruf refers to the qualities of Arabic letters during pronunciation.

These qualities help a learner understand how a letter should sound, where it differs from another letter, and how to avoid changing the meaning of Quranic words through incorrect pronunciation.

Important Sifat Al-Huruf Characteristics

1. Hams — الهمس

Hams means the flow of breath when pronouncing certain letters.

فَحَثَّهُ شَخْصٌ سَكَتْ

These letters are commonly memorized through the phrase above.

2. Jahr — الجهر

Jahr is the opposite of Hams. It refers to letters where the breath does not flow freely during pronunciation.

3. Shiddah — الشدة

Shiddah means strength or restriction of the sound. The sound is strongly stopped when these letters are pronounced.

أَجِدْ قَطٍ بَكَتْ

4. Rikhawah — الرخاوة

Rikhawah means softness or flowing of the sound. Unlike Shiddah, the sound continues when the letter is pronounced.

5. Tawassut — التوسط

Tawassut is an intermediate characteristic between Shiddah and Rikhawah. The sound is neither completely stopped nor completely flowing.

لِنْ عُمَرْ

Qalqalah as a Letter Characteristic

Qalqalah is one of the well-known characteristics of Arabic letters. It produces a slight bouncing or echoing sound when one of the Qalqalah letters is pronounced with Sukoon.

The 5 Qalqalah Letters

ق ط ب ج د

قُطْبُ جَدٍّ

Why Is Sifat Al-Huruf Important?

Learning the characteristics of Arabic letters is important because correct Tajweed is not only about knowing where a letter comes from. You also need to know how that letter should sound.

  • Helps improve Arabic letter pronunciation.
  • Makes similar letters easier to distinguish.
  • Improves Quran recitation accuracy.
  • Helps prevent pronunciation mistakes.
  • Builds a stronger foundation for advanced Tajweed.

Sifat Al-Huruf and Makharij Al-Huruf

Makharij Al-Huruf explains where an Arabic letter comes from, while Sifat Al-Huruf explains the qualities of that letter when it is pronounced.

Easy way to remember:

Makharij = Where the letter comes from.
Sifat = How the letter sounds.

How to Learn Sifat Al-Huruf

  1. Learn the meaning of each characteristic.
  2. Memorize the important Arabic letter groups.
  3. Listen carefully to a qualified Quran teacher.
  4. Practice individual letters before practicing complete words.
  5. Apply the characteristics while reading Quran verses.
  6. Get feedback from a teacher to correct pronunciation mistakes.

💡 Beginner Tip

Do not try to memorize every Tajweed characteristic in one day. Start with the most important groups, practice them with a teacher, and gradually apply them during Quran recitation.
