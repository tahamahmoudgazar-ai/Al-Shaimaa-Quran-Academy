Common Salah Mistakes and How to Correct Them

Learning Salah takes practice. Beginners may make mistakes while learning the prayer movements, recitations, prayer times, or preparation. Making a mistake does not mean that a person should stop learning or become discouraged.

The best approach is to learn Salah gradually, practice consistently, and ask a qualified teacher about specific situations.

🕌 Still learning how to pray?

Start with the basic movements and order of Salah before worrying about small details.

1. Praying Without Proper Purification

One common mistake is beginning Salah without making the required purification. When Wudu is required, it should be completed before prayer.

Beginners should learn which situations require Wudu and which actions invalidate it.

Learn How to Make Wudu →

2. Praying Before the Correct Prayer Time

Each of the five obligatory prayers has its prescribed time. A common mistake is being unsure about when a prayer begins or ends.

Learning the basic prayer times can help Muslims organize their daily prayers.

Learn the Prayer Times in Islam →

3. Forgetting the Number of Rak'ahs

Beginners may sometimes become confused about the number of rak'ahs in a prayer.

The obligatory prayers have different numbers of rak'ahs: Fajr has two, Dhuhr has four, Asr has four, Maghrib has three, and Isha has four.

See How Many Rak'ahs Are in Each Prayer →

4. Rushing Through Salah

Another common problem is performing the movements too quickly. Salah should be performed with calmness and proper composure rather than being rushed.

Beginners should focus on learning each movement correctly and maintaining a steady pace.

5. Not Learning the Basic Recitations

Some beginners focus on the physical movements but do not spend enough time learning what is said during Salah.

Learning Al-Fatihah and the main recitations gradually can make the prayer easier to perform with confidence.

Learn What to Say in Salah →

6. Incorrect Pronunciation of Quranic Recitation

Correct pronunciation is especially important when reciting Quran during Salah. Beginners may struggle with Arabic letters and sounds that do not exist in their native language.

Regular Quran reading practice and guidance from a qualified teacher can help improve pronunciation over time.

7. Forgetting to Complete the Movements

New learners sometimes move quickly from one position to another without properly completing each movement.

Learning the correct sequence of standing, bowing, standing after ruku, prostration, and sitting helps create a consistent prayer routine.

Review the Salah Steps →

8. Being Unsure About the Qiblah

Muslims should face the Qiblah when praying when they are able to do so. Beginners may sometimes be unsure about the direction.

A mosque, reliable Qiblah tool, or knowledgeable local Muslim can help determine the direction.

9. Not Understanding the Conditions of Salah

Some mistakes happen because a beginner does not know the basic conditions that should be fulfilled before prayer.

These include matters such as purification, prayer time, appropriate covering, facing the Qiblah, and intention.

Learn the Conditions of Salah →

10. Getting Discouraged by Small Mistakes

Learning Salah is a process. Beginners should not become discouraged because they are still learning.

Consistent practice, patience, and guidance from a qualified teacher can make a significant difference.

What Should You Do If You Make a Mistake in Salah?

The answer depends on the type of mistake. Some mistakes may require corrective actions, while others may not affect the prayer.

The detailed rulings can differ according to the situation and school of Islamic jurisprudence. If you are unsure whether a particular mistake affects the validity of your prayer, ask a qualified scholar or teacher rather than relying on a general online explanation.

Simple Checklist Before Salah

Check Question
Purification Do I have the required Wudu?
Prayer Time Has the prayer time begun?
Clothing Is my clothing appropriate for Salah?
Qiblah Am I facing the Qiblah?
Intention Do I know which prayer I am performing?
Focus Am I ready to pray calmly?

How to Improve Your Salah

Improving Salah does not require learning everything in one day. A simple learning plan can be more effective.

  1. Learn the conditions and preparation for Salah.
  2. Learn the basic prayer movements.
  3. Memorize and improve the main recitations.
  4. Learn the number of rak'ahs in each obligatory prayer.
  5. Practice regularly.
  6. Ask a qualified teacher to correct pronunciation and movements.
