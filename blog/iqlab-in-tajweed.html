What Is Iqlab in Tajweed? Rule, Letter Baa & Examples

Iqlab is the easiest rule of Noon Sakinah & Tanween. Only ONE letter: Baa (ب). When Noon Sakinah or Tanween meets Baa, Noon turns into Meem with Ghunnah 2 counts. Perfect for USA & UK beginners.

What Is Iqlab? Definition

Iqlab = conversion. Convert Noon sound to Meem sound before Baa. In Mushaf you see small Meem (م) above Noon.

Iqlab Letter - Only Baa ب

Example: مِن بَعْدِ → pronounced Mim-ba'd with Ghunnah, not Min-ba'd.

Iqlab Examples

  • سَمِيعٌ بَصِيرٌ → Samee'um-baseer (Tanween + Baa)
  • أَنبِئْهُمْ → Ambi'hum
  • مِن بَيْنِ → Mim-bayni
