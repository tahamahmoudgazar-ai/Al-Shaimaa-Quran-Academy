What Is Ikhfa in Tajweed? Letters, Rules & Quran Examples

Ikhfa is one of 4 rules of Noon Sakinah & Tanween in Tajweed. It means to hide the Noon sound with Ghunnah when one of 15 Ikhfa letters comes after Noon Sakinah. For USA & UK beginners, Ikhfa is the most common rule in Quran.

What Is Ikhfa? Definition

Ikhfa literally means to conceal. In Tajweed, you don't pronounce Noon clearly nor merge it fully. You make a light nasal sound (Ghunnah) for 2 counts, preparing your tongue for the next letter.

Ikhfa Letters - 15 Letters

The 15 Ikhfa letters are: ت ث ج د ذ ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ف ق ك

Easy sentence to remember: صف ذا ثنا كم جاد شخص قد سما دم طيبا زد في تقى ضع ظالما - first letter of each word.

Ikhfa Examples From Quran

  • مِن تَحْتِهَا - Noon + Ta → Min-tahtiha with Ghunnah
  • أَنفُسَكُمْ - Noon + Fa → Anfusakum
  • مَنثُورًا - Noon + Tha → Manthoora
