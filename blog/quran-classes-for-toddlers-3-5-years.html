Quran Classes for Toddlers 3-5 Years: Gentle Start for Little Muslims

Can a 3-year-old learn Quran? Yes, but differently than older kids. Quran classes for toddlers 3-5 years focus on listening, love, and play - not reading and writing.

Table of Contents

  • What Toddlers Learn

What Toddlers Learn (Age 3-5)

At this age we use Talqeen method - teacher says, child repeats. Similar to how we teach in how to teach kids at home but with teacher support.

  • Arabic sounds through songs
  • Short Surahs: Fatiha, Ikhlas, Nas - from Juz Amma plan
  • Daily Duas
  • Listening skills before learning to read

Ideal Class Length

15-20 minutes only! 2-3 times/week. Longer classes cause loss of focus. Our teachers use whiteboard, puppets, rewards. Learn about how much time kids need for older kids.

Tips for Parents in USA & UK

  • Be present first 2 weeks
  • Repeat same Surah at bedtime - see keep child motivated
  • Use stickers
  • No pressure - just love
  • Balance with play - see balance with school (for older kids but principle same)

When to Start Noorani Qaida?

At 5+ years. For 3-4 focus on oral first. Then start Noorani Qaida online. Also see Quran memorization for kids for next step.
