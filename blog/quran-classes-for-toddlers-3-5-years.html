Quran Classes for Toddlers (3-5 Years): A Gentle Start

For Toddlers

Can a 3-year-old learn Quran? Yes! At 3-5 years, kids learn through play, repetition, and love. Online Quran classes for toddlers focus on Arabic sounds, short Surahs, and Islamic manners - not long lessons.

What Toddlers Learn (Age 3-5)

  • Arabic alphabet through songs and colors
  • Short Surahs: Al-Fatiha, Al-Ikhlas, An-Nas
  • Listening and repeating (Talqeen)
  • Daily Duas and Adab

Ideal Class Length

15-20 minutes only! 2-3 times per week. Longer classes make toddlers lose focus. Our teachers use puppets, whiteboard, and rewards.

Tips for Parents in USA & UK

  • Be present in first classes
  • Repeat same Surah daily at bedtime
  • Use stickers and praise
  • No pressure to read - just listening first

When to Start Noorani Qaida?

Usually at 5+ years. For 3-4 years, focus on oral learning first.
