What is Islamic Studies?

Islamic Studies is the study of Islam as a complete way of life based on Quran and authentic Sunnah. It is not just memorizing information. It is learning what Allah wants from you in belief, worship, manners, and dealings.

Allah says: "Read in the name of your Lord who created" [Quran 96:1]. The first revealed word was "Read" - showing the importance of knowledge in Islam.

Why Islamic Studies is Important for Muslims in the West?

For Muslims in USA and UK, Islamic Studies protects you from 3 dangers: culture mixing with religion, misinformation online, and weak connection with Quran. The Prophet ﷺ said: "When Allah wishes good for someone, He gives him understanding of the religion" [Bukhari & Muslim].

Allah also says: "Say, are those who know equal to those who do not know?" [Quran 39:9]. Knowledge makes your worship correct and your life guided.

7 Main Branches of Islamic Studies

At Al Shimaa Academy, our Islamic Studies course is divided into 7 core subjects. This is exactly what you will learn:

1. Aqeedah (Islamic Creed)

What you must believe about Allah, His Names and Attributes, Angels, Books, Prophets, Day of Judgment, and Qadar. This is the foundation. Without correct Aqeedah, deeds are not accepted. Learn more in our upcoming guide 6 Pillars of Iman.

2. Fiqh (Islamic Jurisprudence)

How to worship correctly - Salah, Zakat, Sawm, Hajj, wudu, halal and haram in daily life. Fiqh answers "How do I do it correctly?" Detailed in Five Pillars of Islam and Fiqh Made Easy.

3. Quran & Tafsir

Understanding Quran, not just reciting. What does Allah mean in each ayah? Why was it revealed? This connects you to Quran deeply. See Introduction to Tafsir.

4. Hadith Sciences

Sayings and actions of Prophet ﷺ. How Hadith was preserved, what is authentic, and how to apply it. Start with Hadith Studies for Beginners.

5. Seerah (Biography of Prophet ﷺ)

Life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in Makkah and Madinah. His character, battles, and lessons for our life today. Full timeline in Seerah Guide.

6. Arabic Language

Key to understand Quran directly without translation. Learn letters, grammar, and Quranic vocabulary. Start from Arabic Language course.

7. Akhlaq & Tazkiyah (Manners & Purification)

How to purify heart, control anger, be honest, be good to parents, and have beautiful character as Muslim in the West.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Islamic Studies and Quran classes?

Quran classes focus on reading, Tajweed, and memorization. Islamic Studies focuses on understanding Islam - Aqeedah, Fiqh, Seerah, Tafsir, Hadith. Both are needed. We recommend doing both together.

Can I learn Islamic Studies online as a complete beginner?

Yes. At Al Shimaa Academy we start from zero. No Arabic or prior knowledge needed. Your teacher will start with basics of Aqeedah and Fiqh of Salah.

How long does Islamic Studies course take?

Our structured program is 12-24 months for foundation level with 2 classes per week. You get certificate and you can continue to advanced levels.

Do you teach kids Islamic Studies online?

Yes, we have specialized curriculum for kids age 7+ with stories from Seerah, basic Fiqh, and Akhlaq taught with patience and engaging methods.
