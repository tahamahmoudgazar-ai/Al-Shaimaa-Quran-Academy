How Much Do Online Quran Classes Cost in USA & UK in 2026?

The cost of online Quran classes can vary considerably from one academy to another. A monthly price by itself does not tell you how much learning time you receive or what is included in the program.

When comparing Quran classes in the USA or UK, parents should look at the complete plan: lesson frequency, lesson duration, private or group format, teacher qualifications, course content and the support provided between lessons.

💡 Quick pricing tip: Do not compare monthly prices alone. Compare the number and length of lessons included in each plan so you can understand the actual value.

What Determines the Cost of Online Quran Classes?

There is no universal price for online Quran lessons. Different academies structure their plans differently, and several factors can change the monthly cost.

1. Number of Classes Per Week

The number of weekly lessons is one of the simplest factors affecting the monthly price. A student attending two lessons per week will normally have fewer teaching hours than a student attending four lessons per week.

Before choosing a plan, decide how much time your child or you can realistically dedicate to Quran learning each week.

2. Lesson Duration

Two plans may both advertise the same number of classes while providing different lesson lengths. For example, a plan with shorter lessons should not be compared directly with a plan offering longer sessions without considering the total teaching time.

Always check whether the advertised price is based on 20-minute, 30-minute, 45-minute or longer lessons.

3. Private or Group Classes

One-to-one lessons and group lessons have different learning formats. In a private lesson, the teacher works directly with one student. Group lessons divide the teacher's attention between several students.

Families should choose the format that matches the student's learning needs, available budget and preferred learning environment.

4. Teacher Qualifications and Experience

Teacher qualifications and teaching experience can also influence how an academy structures its pricing. Parents may want to ask about the teacher's Quran education, Tajweed knowledge and experience teaching children or adults.

The lowest advertised price is not necessarily the best value if the program does not meet the student's learning needs.

5. The Type of Quran Course

The learning goal can also affect the structure of a program. Quran reading, Tajweed, memorization and Islamic Studies may require different lesson plans and different amounts of practice.

For example, a student working on Hifz may need a regular combination of new memorization and revision, while a beginner may need more time to develop basic reading skills.

How to Calculate the Real Monthly Cost

A simple way to compare online Quran programs is to calculate the approximate number of lessons you receive each month and then compare that with the monthly fee.

For example, a schedule of three lessons per week provides roughly twelve lessons in a four-week month. If the monthly plan costs $60, the simple lesson-cost calculation would be:

$60 monthly fee ÷ 12 lessons = $5 per lesson

This calculation is only an example. Actual billing cycles and lesson schedules can vary between academies.

You can use the same method when comparing different plans, but remember to include lesson duration and whether the class is private or shared.

USA vs UK: What Should Families Compare?

Families in the USA and UK may see prices displayed in different currencies or with different billing structures. The important comparison is not simply dollars versus pounds.

Instead, compare:

  • Number of lessons included each week.
  • Length of each lesson.
  • Private or group learning.
  • Teacher qualifications and experience.
  • Course type and learning goals.
  • Schedule flexibility.
  • What support or materials are included.

This approach gives you a better idea of what you are actually paying for.

How Many Classes Per Week Should You Pay For?

The right number of classes depends on the student's goal and available time. More lessons are not automatically better if the student cannot maintain the routine.

  • 2 classes per week: suitable for families looking for a manageable regular routine.
  • 3 classes per week: provides more frequent teacher contact and practice.
  • 4 or more classes per week: may suit students following a more intensive learning or memorization schedule.

For children, parents should also consider school, homework, activities and rest when deciding how many lessons to schedule.

What Should Be Included in a Good Quran Class Plan?

Before paying for an online Quran course, ask exactly what the monthly fee includes.

  • Live instruction with the teacher.
  • The agreed number of lessons.
  • The lesson duration.
  • The selected Quran course or learning program.
  • Progress guidance or feedback, if provided.
  • Any additional learning materials or support.

If an academy does not clearly explain what is included, ask for the complete details before making a payment.

How to Compare Quran Academies by Value

Instead of choosing the cheapest monthly plan, create a simple comparison between the programs you are considering.

  1. Write down the monthly price.
  2. Record the number of lessons per week.
  3. Check the length of each lesson.
  4. Find out whether lessons are private or group-based.
  5. Check teacher qualifications and experience.
  6. Ask whether there is a trial lesson.
  7. Confirm cancellation and payment conditions.

This makes it much easier to see whether a lower monthly price actually provides the type of learning experience you need.

Questions to Ask Before Paying

Parents and adult students can ask these questions before choosing an online Quran academy:

  • How much does the complete monthly plan cost?
  • How many lessons are included?
  • How long is each lesson?
  • Are lessons one-to-one or group classes?
  • Who teaches the course?
  • Can I choose a suitable lesson time?
  • Is there a trial lesson?
  • Are there any additional fees?
  • What happens if I need to change my schedule?

Is a Cheap Online Quran Class Always Better?

Not necessarily. Price is only one part of the decision. A low-cost plan may be useful for a family with a limited budget, but the student should still receive an appropriate lesson schedule, qualified instruction and a course that matches their level.

Likewise, paying more does not automatically guarantee better results. The most useful plan is the one that provides suitable teaching and a schedule the student can maintain consistently.

Frequently Asked Questions About Online Quran Class Costs

How much do online Quran classes cost?

There is no single price for every online Quran class. The monthly cost can depend on the number of lessons, lesson duration, private or group format, teacher and course type. Al Shimaa Quran Academy currently advertises plans starting from $20 per month.

Are online Quran classes cheaper than in-person classes?

It depends on the provider and learning format. Online and in-person programs can have different fees, schedules and class sizes, so it is better to compare the total learning time and services included rather than assuming one format is always cheaper.

Does taking more Quran classes increase the monthly cost?

Usually, a schedule with more teaching hours costs more than a schedule with fewer hours. However, pricing structures vary between academies, so students should check the complete plan before enrolling.

Are one-to-one Quran classes more expensive?

Private lessons may cost more than group classes because the teacher's attention is dedicated to one student. The actual difference depends on the academy, lesson duration and schedule.

Is there an extra fee for Quran classes?

Additional fees depend on the academy and the selected plan. Before paying, ask whether registration, learning materials or other services have separate charges.

Can I try an online Quran class before paying?

A trial lesson can help students and parents evaluate the teacher, lesson format and learning environment before choosing a regular plan. Al Shimaa Quran Academy offers a free trial lesson.
