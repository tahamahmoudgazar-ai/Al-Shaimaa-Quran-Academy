Common Tajweed Mistakes Beginners Make (And How to Fix Them)

Do you read Quran but feel your recitation is not correct? You are not alone. Most beginners make the same common Tajweed mistakes without realizing - and some mistakes can even change the meaning.

In this guide, we will show you the 10 most common Tajweed mistakes beginners make, with examples, and how to fix them with a qualified teacher.

Why Tajweed Mistakes Matter?

Tajweed is not optional. Allah says: "...and recite the Quran with measured recitation (with Tajweed)." [73:4]. A small change in letter or elongation can change meaning completely. Example: Qalb (heart) vs Kalb (dog) - only different because of ق vs ك.

Learn first what Tajweed is and why it is important if you haven't yet.

10 Common Tajweed Mistakes Beginners Make

1. Mixing Heavy and Light Letters (Tafkheem & Tarqeeq)

Mistake: Reading light letters heavy or vice versa. Letters خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ are heavy (mufakhkham). Rest are light.
Example: Saying "Alhamdu" with heavy ل - wrong. ل is light.
Fix: Practice with teacher, mouth position matters.

2. Incorrect Makharij (Articulation Points)

Mistake: Confusing similar letters: س / ص, ذ / ز / ظ, ت / ط, ع / أ.
Example: Reading عَالَمِين with hamza أ instead of ع - changes sound.
Fix: Learn each letter's makhraj from our Noorani Qaida course.

3. Not Giving Ghunnah in Noon & Meem Mushaddad

Mistake: Missing the nasal sound 2 counts for نّ and مّ
Example: إِنَّ - must have 2-count ghunnah
Fix: Hold sound in nose 2 seconds.

4. Qalqalah Mistakes - Bouncing Letters

Mistake: Not bouncing ق ط ب ج د when they have sukoon, or over-bouncing.
Fix: Light bounce, not adding extra vowel.

5. Madd (Elongation) Too Short or Too Long

Mistake: Making 2-count madd 1 count, or 6-count madd 2 counts. Or elongating everywhere.
Example: قَالَ must be 2 counts, not 1.
Fix: Learn types: Natural Madd (2 counts), Wajib Muttasil (4-5), Lazim (6).

6. Ignoring Noon Sakinah & Tanween Rules

Mistake: Not doing Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab, Ikhfa correctly.
Example: مِنْ بَعْدِ must be Iqlab (turn ن to م sound)
Fix: Study 4 rules with examples. See how to improve Quran reading.

7. Rushing and No Tarteel

Mistake: Reading too fast to finish quickly.
Fix: Prophet ﷺ read slowly, letter by letter. Slow is better than fast with mistakes.

8. Not Stopping at Correct Waqf (Stopping Signs)

Mistake: Stopping in middle of meaning, or not breathing at correct place.
Fix: Learn Waqf signs: م (must stop), لا (don't stop), ج (allowed).

9. Same Mistake in Salah

Mistake: Making Tajweed mistakes in Fajr and other prayers invalidates beauty of salah. Many new Muslims struggle with Al-Fatiha.
Fix: Master Al-Fatiha first - it is obligatory in salah. Review how to pray Fajr and recite slowly.

10. Learning Tajweed from YouTube Only Without Teacher

Mistake: Biggest mistake - Tajweed cannot be learned only from videos. You need a teacher to listen and correct you.
Fix: Book evaluation with qualified Egyptian teacher with Ijazah.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my prayer be invalid if I make Tajweed mistakes?

If mistake changes meaning and you do it intentionally, some scholars say prayer may be invalid. If unintentional as beginner learning, prayer is valid but you must keep learning. Start with perfecting Al-Fatiha.

How long does it take to fix Tajweed mistakes?

With daily 30 mins practice and weekly teacher correction, most beginners fix basic mistakes in 2-3 months. Complete Tajweed mastery takes 1-2 years. See how long it takes to memorize Quran for long term goals.

Do I need a female Quran teacher?

Many sisters prefer female teachers. We have qualified female Quran teachers online with Ijazah for sisters and kids.

What is the best way to learn Tajweed online?
