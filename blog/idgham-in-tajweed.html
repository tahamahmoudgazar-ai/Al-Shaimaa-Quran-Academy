What Is Idgham in Tajweed? Types, Letters & Quran Examples

Idgham means to merge Noon Sakinah or Tanween into one of 6 Idgham letters. It's one of 4 Noon Sakinah rules. For beginners in USA & UK, Idgham makes Quran recitation smooth and connected.

What Is Idgham? Definition

Idgham = merging. You merge Noon sound into next letter so they become one emphasized letter. Sometimes with nasal sound (Ghunnah), sometimes without.

Idgham Letters - 6 Letters - يرملون

All Idgham letters in one word: يَرْمَلُون (Ya, Ra, Meem, Lam, Waw, Noon)

2 Types of Idgham

  • Idgham with Ghunnah (4 letters): ي ن م و - Yanmu - with nasal 2 counts - Example: مَن يَعْمَلُ → Mai-ya'mal
  • Idgham without Ghunnah (2 letters): ل ر - No nasal - Example: مِن لَّدُنْهُ → Mil-ladunhu
