Why Learn Egyptian Arabic Dialect?

1. Most Understood Dialect in Arab World

Due to Egyptian cinema (Cairo is Hollywood of Middle East), Egyptian dialect is understood from Morocco to Saudi Arabia. If you learn Egyptian, you can communicate almost everywhere.

2. Perfect for Travel to Egypt

Planning travel to Egypt – Cairo, Luxor, Sharm El Sheikh? MSA won't help you in taxi, market, restaurant. Egyptian Ammeya will. Learn phrases like Ezzayak? (How are you?), Bekam dah? (How much is this?), Fin el hotel? (Where is hotel?)

3. For Family, Spouse & Friends in USA

Many Egyptian families live in USA, UK, Canada. If your spouse, friends or in-laws are Egyptian, learning Egyptian dialect shows love and respect and helps daily communication at home.

4. Understand Egyptian Movies & Songs Without Subtitles

Love Egyptian movies, series like El Ekhteyar, and singers like Amr Diab, Umm Kulthum? Egyptian dialect course helps you understand without subtitles.
