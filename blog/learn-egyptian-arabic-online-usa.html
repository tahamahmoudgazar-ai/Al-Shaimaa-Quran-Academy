Why Learn Egyptian Arabic Dialect in USA?

1. Most Understood: Cairo is Hollywood of Middle East. Egyptian is understood from Morocco to Saudi Arabia.
2. Travel to Egypt: MSA won't help in taxi/market. Ammeya will: Ezzayak? Bekam dah? Fin el hotel?
3. Family in USA: Many Egyptians in USA/UK. Learn for spouse, in-laws, friends.
4. Movies & Songs: Understand Amr Diab, Umm Kulthum, Egyptian series without subtitles.

Common Egyptian Phrases for USA Learners

  • Ezzayak? / Ezzayek? = How are you? (m/f)
  • 3amel eh? = What are you doing?
  • El hamdulellah kwais = Good
  • Bekam dah? – Ghali awi! = How much? Too expensive!
  • Yalla bina – Mashi – Tamam = Let's go – Okay
  • Bahebak / Bahebek = I love you
