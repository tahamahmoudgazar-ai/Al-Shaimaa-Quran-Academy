Egyptian vs MSA – Quick Difference

MSA: Kayfa halak? Kam thamanuhu?
Egyptian: Ezzayak? Bekam dah?
Egyptian is shorter, faster, what Egyptians really speak.

Common Egyptian Phrases for USA Learners

  • Ezzayak? / Ezzayek? / 3amel eh? = How are you?
  • El hamdulellah kwais = Good
  • Bekam dah? – Ghali awi! = How much? Too expensive!
  • Yalla bina – Mashi – Tamam = Let's go – Okay
  • Fin el hotel? 3ayez aruh... = Where is hotel? I want to go...
