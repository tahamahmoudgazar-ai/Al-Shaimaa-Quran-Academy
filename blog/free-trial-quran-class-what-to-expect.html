Before Your Free Trial Quran Class

You do not need extensive preparation. A few simple steps can help you get started comfortably.

  • Check the joining instructions: Read the booking confirmation carefully for the meeting link, platform and lesson time.
  • Test your device: Check your internet connection, microphone and speakers before the session.
  • Choose a quiet place: Find a comfortable space where you can listen and practice recitation.
  • Bring your learning materials: Keep a Quran or suitable reading material and a notebook nearby if needed.
  • Think about your goals: Decide whether you want to improve reading, learn Tajweed, memorize Quran or begin with Arabic letters.

If you are new to online lessons, our guide on how to learn Quran online explains useful ways to approach remote learning.

What Happens During a Trial Class?

The exact format and duration may vary. A trial lesson may include a conversation about your goals, a brief review of your current level, a sample learning activity and a discussion of possible next steps.

  1. Discuss your goals: Explain whether the lessons are for you or your child and describe any previous Quran learning experience.
  2. Review your current level: The teacher may ask you to read a short passage or demonstrate a skill relevant to your goals.
  3. Try a learning activity: Depending on your needs, the session may introduce Quran reading, Noorani Qaida, Tajweed or memorization.
  4. Discuss a learning plan: Ask about suitable lesson frequency, learning priorities and ways to practice between classes.

If your goal is to improve Quran recitation, read our beginner-friendly explanation of what Tajweed is and why it matters.

Questions to Ask Your Quran Teacher

A trial is also a chance to decide whether the teaching arrangement meets your needs. Consider asking these questions before choosing regular lessons.

  • What learning plan would suit my current level or my child's level?
  • Which subjects can I study: Quran reading, Tajweed, Hifz, Noorani Qaida or Islamic Studies?
  • How often should lessons take place to support steady progress?
  • What should I practice between lessons?
  • What happens if I need to reschedule or miss a class?
  • Are female teachers available if that is my preference?
  • What lesson schedules are available for my time zone?

Parents may also find our guide to choosing an online Quran teacher for a child useful.

Choosing the Right Lessons for Your Needs

The best learning plan depends on the student's age, current skills and goals. A beginner may need to start with Arabic letters and foundational reading, while someone who already reads Quran may want to focus on Tajweed or memorization.

  • For children: Consider age-appropriate lessons and a manageable practice routine.
  • For adult beginners: Ask how lessons can start from the basics and progress step by step.
  • For Tajweed learners: Discuss pronunciation, recitation rules and how progress will be reviewed.
  • For memorization: Ask about revision, realistic goals and a consistent practice schedule.
  • For sisters: If you prefer a female teacher, ask about availability before confirming regular lessons.

Frequently Asked Questions

Al Shimaa Quran Academy advertises a free trial. Check the registration page for the current booking details and any applicable conditions.

Do I need to prepare before the trial?

Extensive preparation is not necessary. Check the joining instructions, test your device, find a quiet place and think about what you want to learn.

What should children have ready for a trial?

Help your child join from a quiet space with a suitable device and any requested learning materials. Parents can share the child's age, experience and learning goals when arranging the session.

Can I ask for a female Quran teacher?

If you prefer a female teacher, mention this when booking and ask about current availability. You can read more in our guide to female Quran teachers online.

How do I find the right plan after the trial?

Consider your goals, available schedule and recommended lesson frequency. Review the current pricing options and ask the academy to clarify anything you are unsure about before registering.
