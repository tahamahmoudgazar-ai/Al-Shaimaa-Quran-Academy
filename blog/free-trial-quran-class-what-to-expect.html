Getting Started

Booked a free trial? Here's exactly what happens in your first free Quran class at Al Shimaa Academy and how to prepare in 5 minutes.

Before the Trial (2 Minutes Setup)

  • We send Zoom/Google Meet link
  • No software download needed
  • Test mic and camera
  • Have a notebook ready

During the 30-Minute Trial

  1. Teacher introduces and assesses your level (5 min)
  2. Short demo lesson - Noorani Qaida or Tajweed (15 min)
  3. Learning plan & pricing explanation (10 min)

Questions to Ask Teacher

  • What plan do you recommend for me/my child?
  • How many classes per week?
  • What if we miss a class?
  • Can we change teacher if needed?
