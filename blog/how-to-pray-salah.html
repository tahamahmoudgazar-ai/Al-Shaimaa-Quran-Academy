How to Pray Salah: Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners

Learning how to pray Salah is an important step for every Muslim. Although prayer may seem difficult when you first begin, its structure becomes easier with regular practice.

This beginner-friendly guide explains the basic steps of Salah, from preparing for prayer to completing the final Taslim.

What Do You Need Before Salah?

Before beginning prayer, a Muslim should make sure that the required conditions of Salah are fulfilled.

  • Prayer time has begun.
  • You have the required state of purification.
  • Your body and clothing meet the requirements of cleanliness.
  • The required parts of the body are covered.
  • You face the Qiblah when able.
  • You have the intention to perform the prayer.

If you need help with purification, see our step-by-step guide to making Wudu .

How to Pray Salah Step by Step

1. Make the Intention

Know which prayer you are about to perform and make the intention for that prayer.

2. Face the Qiblah

Stand facing the direction of the Ka'bah in Makkah.

3. Begin With Takbir

Begin the prayer with Takbir, declaring the greatness of Allah.

4. Stand and Recite

While standing, recite the required Quranic passages, including Surah Al-Fatihah.

5. Perform Ruku'

Bow in the prescribed manner and perform the appropriate remembrance.

6. Return to Standing

Rise from Ruku' and stand upright before continuing.

7. Perform Sujud

Prostrate twice in each rak'ah while making the prescribed remembrance.

8. Complete the Rak'ahs

Continue the same basic sequence until you complete the required number of rak'ahs for your prayer.

9. Tashahhud and Final Sitting

Sit and recite the required testimony and supplications at the appropriate point in the prayer.

10. Finish With Taslim

Complete the prayer with Taslim.

How Many Rak'ahs Should You Pray?

The obligatory rak'ahs are:

  • Fajr: 2
  • Dhuhr: 4
  • Asr: 4
  • Maghrib: 3
  • Isha: 4

For more details, read our guide: How Many Rak'ahs Are in Each Prayer?

Common Mistakes Beginners Make

  • Rushing through the prayer.
  • Not learning the correct Arabic pronunciation.
  • Forgetting the number of rak'ahs.
  • Not learning the prayer times.
  • Not learning the requirements of Wudu.
  • Trying to memorize everything at once.

How Can Beginners Learn Salah More Easily?

The best approach is to learn gradually. Start by learning the movements and essential recitations, then practice each prayer regularly.

A qualified teacher can also correct pronunciation and movement mistakes before they become habits.
