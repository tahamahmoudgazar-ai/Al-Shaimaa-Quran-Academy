Everything you need to know about learning Quran online

Who can join Al Shimaa Quran Academy?

Anyone, anywhere in the world can join! Our courses are designed for kids, adults, beginners, and advanced learners, sisters and new Muslims. All you need is a device and internet connection. We serve students across USA, UK, Canada, Europe, Australia.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, we offer a 100% free trial class so you can experience our quality before committing. No credit card needed. Book your free trial here.

What courses do you offer?

We offer Quran Reading, Quran Memorization (Hifz), Tajweed, Arabic Language, and Islamic Studies, with customized programs for each age and level. See all courses | Read our guides including What is Tajweed.

Can I choose my class timings?

Yes, we offer flexible scheduling. You can pick the days and times that suit your family's routine, wherever you live in the world. We support EST, PST, GMT and all time zones for USA, UK, Canada families.

Are the classes live or pre-recorded?

All our classes are 100% live with real teachers. You'll get one-to-one attention and real-time feedback. Each session is interactive via Zoom. Our teachers are Al-Azhar certified male and female instructors.

How much do Quran lessons cost?

Our pricing is flexible and affordable, starting from just $20/month depending on your plan, course type, and frequency. We offer custom packages for individuals and families with up to 20% discount. Check pricing here and contact us for a free quote.

Is there a refund guarantee?

Yes! We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with your experience, you can request a refund. Learn more in our beginners guide or FAQ page.
