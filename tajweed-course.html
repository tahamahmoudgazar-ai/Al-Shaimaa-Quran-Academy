Learn Quran with Tajweed Online

Tajweed is the set of rules that helps Muslims recite the Quran correctly and clearly. Learning Tajweed can improve pronunciation, articulation and the accuracy of Quran recitation.

Online Tajweed Course FAQ

Common questions about learning Quran Tajweed online

What is Tajweed?

Tajweed is the collection of rules used to help Muslims pronounce Quranic letters correctly and recite the Quran accurately.

Can beginners learn Tajweed online?

Yes. Beginners can start with the basic pronunciation and Tajweed rules and gradually develop their Quran recitation skills.

Can children learn Tajweed?

Yes. Tajweed lessons can be adapted to children according to their age, Quran reading level and learning ability.

Are the Tajweed classes live?

Yes. Our online Tajweed lessons are live and interactive, allowing students to receive real-time guidance and pronunciation corrections from their teacher.

Can I combine Tajweed with Quran memorization?

Yes. Tajweed and Quran memorization can complement each other. Correct recitation is an important part of building strong Quran memorization skills.
