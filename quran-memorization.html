Quran Memorization Course FAQ

Common questions about our online Quran Hifz program

Can children learn Quran memorization online?

Yes. Children can follow a structured Quran memorization program with a qualified teacher and a learning pace appropriate for their age.

Do I need to know how to read Quran before starting Hifz?

Good Quran reading and pronunciation skills are important for memorization. Beginners who need reading support can work on their Quran reading skills before or alongside their memorization program.

How often should I review memorized Quran?

Regular revision is an important part of Quran memorization. Your teacher can help you create a review routine based on the amount you have memorized and your personal schedule.

Can adults memorize the Quran online?

Yes. Adults can follow a flexible Quran memorization program designed around their available time, current level and personal goals.
