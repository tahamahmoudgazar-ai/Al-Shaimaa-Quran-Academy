Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

At Al Shimaa Quran Academy (alshimaaquranacademy.com), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information and your children's information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you register for a free trial: Name, Email, Phone (WhatsApp), Country, Timezone (EST/PST/GMT), and learning goals. We also collect payment information via secure third-party processors (we do not store card data).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule your Quran classes and match you with a qualified teacher (male/female)
  • To communicate via Email and WhatsApp about lessons
  • To improve our services and support

3. Children's Privacy

We provide Quran classes for kids. We only collect children's information with parental consent. We never share children's data for marketing purposes. Parents can request deletion of their child's data anytime via contact@alshimaaquranacademy.com

4. Cookies

We use cookies for Google Analytics and to improve user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share data with essential services: Zoom (for live classes), Google Analytics, and payment gateways.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at: contact@alshimaaquranacademy.com
