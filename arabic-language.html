Online Arabic Course FAQ

Common questions about learning Arabic online

Can beginners learn Arabic online?

Yes. Beginners can start Arabic from the alphabet and basic vocabulary. Lessons are adapted to the student's current level.

Are Arabic classes available for children?

Yes. Arabic lessons can be customized for children according to their age, learning level and educational goals.

What will I learn in the Arabic course?

Depending on your level, lessons may include Arabic letters, reading, writing, vocabulary, grammar and everyday conversation.

Are the Arabic lessons live?

Yes. Our online Arabic lessons are live and interactive, allowing students to communicate directly with their teacher and receive real-time feedback.

Can Arabic lessons help me understand Quranic Arabic?

Yes. Learning Arabic vocabulary and basic language structures can help students develop a stronger foundation for understanding common Quranic words and expressions.
